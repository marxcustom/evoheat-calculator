FYI for best results ensure:

  • The vessel is appropriately well insulated.
  • All pipework and filter are appropriately lagged/ insulated.
  • The heat pump is not drawing in hot air from any equipment within its vicinity.
  • Use blanket/covers when not in use to retain chill.
  • Particularly for inground plunge pools, ensure thermal barriers are used between cold and hot bodies of water in close proximity.
  • Face any heat pump used for heating water, towards the chiller to draw in its cold waste air discharge.
